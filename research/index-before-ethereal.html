Life can ask you to begin again. You deserve someone beside you as you do.

Déa’s work is personal. As a mother of five, a Brazilian-born adoptee, and a woman who has navigated divorce, she brings lived experience to conversations about change.

Her coaching centers on women’s empowerment, marriage, family, and relationships. Her own journey is rooted in faith, and her door is open to people of every background, with faith or without.

Before we talk

Is this for me?

Déa works with women, men, and couples. Her areas of focus include women’s empowerment, marriage, family, and life after divorce. A consultation is a place to discuss whether her approach fits what you’re looking for.

Do I need to share her faith?

No. Faith is part of Déa’s personal story, and she welcomes people of all backgrounds, including those with no religious faith.

How do I get started?

Choose a consultation on Déa’s booking page or send her a message. You can share a little about what brings you here, even if you don’t have all the words yet.

Where are the details?

The official booking and mentorship pages have current availability, session arrangements, and pricing. You can visit them directly from each option above.
